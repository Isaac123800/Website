What Is Freedom of Opinion?

Freedom of opinion means that people have the right to hold their own thoughts, beliefs and viewpoints.

A healthy society allows people to have different opinions and encourages respectful discussion and open-mindedness.

Why Does It Matter?

🗣️

Expression

People should be able to share their ideas, thoughts and opinions.

🤝

Respect

We can disagree with others while still treating them with respect.

🌍

Diversity

Different perspectives can help us understand the world in new ways.

Different Opinions, One Community

We do not always have to agree. Listening to different perspectives can help us learn and understand each other.

"Different opinions can exist without losing respect for one another."
